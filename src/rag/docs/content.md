# Handbook 

Day 1 in the new town of Cedar Ridge. The movers finally finished unloading the truck around 8 PM. It’s quiet here compared to the city, almost too quiet. Maya helped me unpack the kitchen boxes while we ate cold pizza on the floor. I still can't believe we found a place with an actual fireplace and a backyard for Cooper to run around in.

Took Cooper, our Golden Retriever, to the local vet near the square today for his annual checkup. Dr. Aris said he’s in great shape, though he gained two pounds since last fall. He recommended switching Cooper to a lower-calorie kibble and increasing his daily walks along the lake trail.

First official day working remotely as a data analyst for Northwind Tech. Setup took hours because the Wi-Fi kept dropping in the spare bedroom office. Spent most of the morning in orientation on Zoom with my team lead, Sarah. She seems nice and mentioned that the team usually does virtual coffee chats every Thursday at 10 AM.

Needed a break from the home office so I walked down to the square and tried a local cafe called The Rusty Mug. The barista recommended their iced oat milk latte, which was surprisingly good. I sat by the window for two hours finishing the quarterly report for Northwind Tech while Cooper waited patiently at home with Maya.

The garden project is officially a disaster. Planted tomatoes, basil, and bell peppers in the backyard raised bed last weekend, but a sudden frost overnight killed almost all the seedlings. Maya laughed and said we should stick to buying produce at the Saturday farmer's market instead.

Spent the weekend camping at Maple Lake, about 45 minutes north of Cedar Ridge. We rented a small wooden cabin right by the water. Cooper spent hours swimming after sticks until he was completely exhausted. At night, Maya built a campfire and we roasted marshmallows under the stars.

Started working on a small side project in Python tonight after dinner. I’m building a script to automate organizing my photo collection by date and location metadata. It's been a while since I wrote code purely for personal fun rather than work task tickets at Northwind.

Bad news today—the Subaru's check engine light came on while driving back from the grocery store. Took it to Miller's Auto Shop and the mechanic told me the alternator needs to be replaced. Total cost will be around $450. Between that and the Maple Lake trip last month, budget is going to be tight this June.

Celebrated Maya’s big news tonight! She officially got promoted to Senior Designer at her firm. We took a group of friends out to dinner at Bella Italia in town. Ordered a bottle of red wine and shared their signature tiramisu for dessert. Super proud of all the hard work she put into the brand redesign project.

Heatwave hit Cedar Ridge hard this week it hit 96 degrees today! The air conditioning in the house ran non-stop. Took Cooper out for a late walk near Maple Lake around 8:30 PM once the sun went down so his paws wouldn't burn on the hot asphalt. Ran into our neighbor Tom, who invited us over for a barbecue this coming Sunday.
